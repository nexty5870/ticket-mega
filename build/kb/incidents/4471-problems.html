#4471 was marked known: one issue, one fix queued. Its reports said otherwise. Read one by one, they describe more than one bug. Each problem below has its own ticket, with its own count.

Engineering closed #4471 on 5 October. parser is fixed (fix(dates): parse dd/mm for eu locales). hint won’t be fixed: the booking-v3 form is retired. Nothing in that update touches shift.

#4471 is closed, but shift is not fixed. A date that moves by one day goes to its own ticket, not to #4471.

The counts follow the desk’s own view, as exported. The export’s pages overlap, so its rows (427) hold 412 reports. Each report is filed under the cause its tenant and the way it came in point to (causes.ts), not under its intake tag: intake guesses from the reporter’s browser, and the table shows how often that guess is wrong. shift reports stay filed under parser as well, so the rows add up to more than the total.

parser: the shared parser reads dates month first

It covers 379 reports. Every tenant on file writes the day first. The shared parser read every date month first, so a booking for 7 April went out on 4 July. This is the bug #4471 was opened for, and it is fixed. Reports from booking-v3 tenants count here when they came in by app or by email.

hint: booking-v3’s form tells day-first people to type the month first

It covers 33 reports, all through the booking form of tenants that run booking-v3. That form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People type what it says, and the form reads the date the tenant’s way, day first. The date is wrong before the parser ever sees it, so the parser fix does not touch this one. The form is retired, so the ticket closes as won’t fix.

shift: the date moves by one day

It covers 5 reports. Here the month does not flip; the day moves by one:

  • “Invite says Tuesday 12 May. Calendar says Monday 11 May.”
  • “Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday. Same meeting.”
  • “The invite arrived before I made the booking.”

That reads as a time zone bug: a date stored as a moment in one zone and shown in another. The desk noted these as one-day moves (desk-notes.json) and files them under parser by tenant. The parser fix does not move them back, and closing #4471 did not close them.

Keeping this page right

The numbers on this page are computed when it renders, from reports-4471.json and desk-notes.json. When the desk merges more reports, or notes another one-day move, the table and the counts follow. A report from a tenant that is not on file shows up as unplaced.

Lessons

  • Same subject is not the same bug. Decision 0001 merges on subject alone and expects engineering to split later. Here nobody split, and the ticket was marked known.
  • Before you mark a big ticket known, read its reports, not only its subject lines.
  • A closed ticket is not a fixed problem. Check each problem against what the fix touched.
