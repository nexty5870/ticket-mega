#4471 is marked known: one issue, one fix queued. Its reports say otherwise. Read one by one, they describe more than one bug, and the queued parser fix closes only one of them. Each problem below has its own ticket now, with its own count.

The parser fix does not close the other problems. Keep #4471 open until every ticket below is fixed.

Each report counts once. The export’s pages overlap, so 427 rows hold 412 reports. A report’s problem comes from its tenant and the way it came in (causes.ts), not from the intake tag: intake guesses from the reporter’s browser, and the two columns above show how often that guess is wrong.

parser: the shared parser reads dates month first

It covers 374 reports. Every tenant on file writes the day first. The shared parser reads every date month first, so a booking for 7 April goes out on 4 July. This is the bug #4471 was opened for, and the queued fix is for it. Reports from booking-v3 tenants count here when they came in by app or by email.

hint: booking-v3’s form tells day-first people to type the month first

It covers 33 reports, all through the booking form of tenants that run booking-v3. That form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People type what it says, and the form reads the date the tenant’s way, day first. The date is wrong before the parser ever sees it, so the parser fix does not touch this one. #4471’s own table already marks it “not this ticket — no ticket of its own”.

shift: the date moves by one day

It covers 5 reports. Here the month does not flip; the day moves by one:

  • “Invite says Tuesday 12 May. Calendar says Monday 11 May.”
  • “Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday. Same meeting.”
  • “The invite arrived before I made the booking.”

That reads as a time zone bug: a date stored as a moment in one zone and shown in another. The desk noted these as one-day moves (desk-notes.json), but #4471 still counts them under parser. The parser fix does not move them back.

Keeping this page right

The numbers on this page are computed when it renders, from reports-4471.json and desk-notes.json. When the desk merges more reports into #4471, or notes another one-day move, the table and the counts follow. A report from a tenant that is not on file shows up as unplaced.

Lessons

  • Same subject is not the same bug. Decision 0001 merges on subject alone and expects engineering to split later. Here nobody split, and the ticket was marked known.
  • Before you mark a big ticket known, read its reports, not only its subject lines.
